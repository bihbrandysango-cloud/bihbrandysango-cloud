# Hi, I'm Bih Brandy Sango

I'm a data scientist and BI analyst based in Limbe, Cameroon. I take messy business data and turn it into dashboards and reports that someone can act on the same week. Most of my work is in Power BI, SQL and Excel, and I'm a 2026 Women Techsters Fellow (Tech4Dev) on the Data Science & Engineering track.
